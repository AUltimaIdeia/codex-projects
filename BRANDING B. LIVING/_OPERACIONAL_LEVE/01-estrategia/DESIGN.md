# Design System: B. Living Floripa

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Decision Table"**

B. Living uses a quiet, strategic visual system. The surface should feel like a premium decision room: deep, precise, editorial and controlled. The brand does not look like a real estate shelf, a construction company, a beach resort or a luxury cliche.

The system translates authority into structure. Rafael, equipe, metodo, cidade and curadoria appear as proof signals before any product. Visual density is allowed only when it suggests analysis, map, grid, plan or decision logic.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Deep navy as the dominant recognition color.
- Warm off-white typography and logo applications.
- Brass details only as fine rules, never as luxury decoration.
- Editorial typography for headlines, clean sans for explanation.
- Flat tonal layering instead of shadows or glass effects.
- Strong hierarchy, generous negative space and controlled grids.

## 2. Colors

The palette is dark, mineral and restrained: premium by depth and silence, not by shine.

### Primary

- **Deep Decision Navy** (`oklch(16.5% 0.048 245)`): main background, brand atmosphere and institutional surfaces.
- **Night Navy** (`oklch(12.5% 0.035 250)`): deeper background layer, edge darkness and vignette.
- **Soft Navy** (`oklch(22% 0.048 240)`): secondary dark surface for panels, maps and analytical blocks.

### Secondary

- **Territory Petrol** (`oklch(42% 0.055 205)`): restrained support color for territory, city and map logic.
- **Stone Graphite** (`oklch(37% 0.012 238)`): institutional neutral for dividers, muted panels and low-contrast surfaces.

### Tertiary

- **Quiet Brass** (`oklch(68% 0.055 78)`): fine rule, marker, sequencing accent and small proof detail.
- **Soft Brass** (`oklch(76% 0.045 80)`): lighter brass for numbers, labels and small emphasis.

### Neutral

- **Warm Paper** (`oklch(92% 0.015 82)`): logo on dark, main text, light panels and editorial contrast.
- **Soft Paper** (`oklch(84% 0.018 82)`): body text on dark backgrounds.
- **Dim Paper** (`oklch(70% 0.018 82)`): labels, captions and secondary navigation.

### Named Rules

**The Navy First Rule.** The brand should be recognized first by deep navy and warm paper, not by gold.

**The Brass Restraint Rule.** Brass may appear as a line, dot or small label. It must not become a dominant fill, gradient or metallic effect.

## 3. Typography

**Primary / Display Font:** Bodoni Moda, Bodoni 72, Didot, Bodoni MT, Georgia, Times New Roman, serif  
**Secondary / Body Font:** Montserrat, Avenir Next, Helvetica Neue, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif  
**Label Font:** Montserrat, Avenir Next, Helvetica Neue, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif

**Character:** The typography follows the approved reference image: a high-contrast didone serif for authority, materiality and editorial presence; paired with a clean tracked sans for captions, labels and consultive explanation.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (400, 202px, 0.92): manifesto headlines and high-impact brand statements.
- **Headline** (400, 74px, 0.96): institutional cards, final statements and major section anchors.
- **Title** (400, 64px, 0.98): pillar names, proof blocks and compact editorial titles.
- **Body** (370, 42px, 1.26): consultive explanation, manifesto support copy and decision rationale.
- **Label** (650, 23px, 0.08em, uppercase): short institutional labels, sequence numbers and map captions only.

### Named Rules

**The Didone Authority Rule.** Major brand statements should use the primary high-contrast serif with generous spacing and controlled line breaks. It should feel architectural, not decorative.

**The Serif Uppercase Rule.** In generated images, landing previews, covers, posts, headers and validation tests, every display serif text block must be uppercase. Do not use mixed case or lowercase for didone/display statements.

**The Secondary Precision Rule.** Supporting text, captions and labels use the secondary sans. Labels may use wide tracking, but body copy must stay comfortable and readable.

**The Short Sentence Rule.** Headlines should be short enough to feel like a strategic position, not a sales pitch.

**The Label Discipline Rule.** Uppercase is reserved for short labels, sequence markers and display serif statements. It is not used for paragraphs.

## 4. Elevation

This system is flat by default. Depth is created with tonal contrast, borders, grid layers, texture and composition. Shadows are avoided because they can make the brand feel like a generic digital template.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Inset structure** (`inset 0 0 0 1px oklch(0% 0 0 / 0.18)`): allowed inside analytical/map panels when a surface needs more precision.

### Named Rules

**The No Floating Card Rule.** Do not use drop shadows to make the brand feel premium. Use spacing, border, color and hierarchy.

## 5. Components

Components should feel like brand surfaces, not app widgets. They are quiet, rectangular, editorial and structured.

### Buttons

- **Shape:** square or minimally rounded (`0px` to `4px`).
- **Primary:** deep navy background, warm paper text, thin warm paper or brass border.
- **Hover / Focus:** slight tonal shift or border emphasis. No glow, bounce or glass.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** transparent dark background with warm paper text and 1px border.

### Chips

- **Style:** small uppercase labels in warm paper or brass, with wide letter spacing.
- **State:** use only for filters, territories or proof tags. Avoid colorful pill clusters.

### Cards / Containers

- **Corner Style:** square by default.
- **Background:** deep navy, soft navy or warm paper depending on hierarchy.
- **Shadow Strategy:** no shadow at rest. Use tonal layering and 1px borders.
- **Border:** 1px low-opacity warm paper or navy.
- **Internal Padding:** generous, usually `56px` to `78px` in high-resolution social compositions.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** flat, quiet, high-contrast enough to be readable.
- **Focus:** border shift to brass or warm paper.
- **Error / Disabled:** muted stone/graphite, never red-heavy unless required by a functional interface.

### Navigation

Navigation should be sparse and institutional. Use short labels, warm paper on dark navy, and avoid oversized buttons, round pills or decorative icons.

### Signature Component

**Decision Map.** A thin-line analytical panel that suggests city, timing, product logic and curated judgment. Use straight lines, rectangles, coordinates and dots. Do not use waves or decorative patterns.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use deep navy as the dominant brand field.
- **Do** use the real B. Living logos from `brandbook-toolkit/02-Logos`.
- **Do** place Rafael and equipe before bairros, imoveis or product.
- **Do** use grid, line, map and decision-table logic to suggest method.
- **Do** keep copy consultive: criterio, leitura, confianca, patrimonio, timing.
- **Do** use brass as a fine accent, never as the main premium signal.
- **Do** create hierarchy through scale, silence and composition.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use ondas, wave patterns or nautical motifs as the brand system.
- **Don't** make the brand look like resort, clube nautico, construtora or imobiliaria comum.
- **Don't** rely on gold, metallic effects or excess brass to signal alto padrao.
- **Don't** use generic real estate phrases such as imperdivel, incrivel, ultimas unidades or chame agora.
- **Don't** turn Instagram into a catalog of properties without tese.
- **Don't** use glassmorphism, gradient text, bokeh or decorative orbs.
- **Don't** use large rounded cards, icon grids or template-like social layouts.
