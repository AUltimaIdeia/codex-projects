# Prompt Imagegen - Post 01 Base Visual

Uso: gerar a base visual raster do Post 01. O texto final e a logo oficial devem ser aplicados depois em layout, para preservar fidelidade tipografica e uso correto do SVG oficial.

## Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: Instagram carousel visual base, square 1:1
Primary request: Create a premium editorial visual base for B. Living Floripa's Instagram carousel manifesto about the idea "Antes do imovel, vem a decisao". Do not render the phrase as text. The image should communicate that before showing a property, there is reading, criterion, patrimonial decision, city analysis and trust.
Scene/backdrop: Florianopolis seen as a strategic territory, not a tourist postcard. Dark, sophisticated city/coastline atmosphere, subtle architecture, a decision table or dossier surface in the foreground with abstract map lines, thin analytical marks, mineral texture, architectural plan fragments and warm paper details.
Subject: Real estate curatorship as decision-making: city, property, patrimony, timing and criterion. No people posing.
Style/medium: cinematic premium editorial photography, quiet authority, institutional luxury without ostentation.
Composition/framing: square composition with generous negative space for later typography. The visual should work as a carousel cover and as a reusable background for interior slides. Strong left or center field for large title text, subtle detail around edges.
Lighting/mood: low-key cinematic light, deep blue night mood, warm off-white paper glow, restrained brass highlights.
Color palette: deep saturated navy blue dominant, warm off-white, graphite, petroleum blue, minimal aged brass.
Materials/textures: mineral stone, matte paper, fine lines, architectural drawing texture, subtle city lights.
Text: no text, no letters, no fake logo, no signage, no watermark.
Constraints: leave clear negative space for typography; premium editorial; controlled, sober, precise; no visible brand imitation.
Avoid: beach tourism, waves, nautical symbols, resort feeling, obvious luxury, excessive gold, keys, smiling salesperson, generic real estate catalog, construction company render, promotional ad look, stock-photo look, fake B. Living logo, readable text.
